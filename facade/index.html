How to draw

Every part snaps to real sizes, so the facade you draw is a measured drawing. Sizes are in feet and inches (metres are in the menu).

  1. Place parts. Pick Wall, Roof, Window or another part, then tap the board to drop one, or drag to draw it at any size. A roof tapped above a wall fits itself to that wall.
  2. Select and adjust. With Select, tap a part, drag it to move, and drag its round handles to resize. Tap a part twice, or the sliders button, to type exact sizes, change its style, texture and color, repeat it, mirror it or add its dimensions.
  3. Select several. With Select, drag across empty board to box-select, or turn on Multi and tap parts to add them. Then move them together, group them, align and distribute them, or copy and paste them into the other view or another design.
  4. Snap. Grid snaps to the grid size. Snap lines parts up with the edges and centers of other parts and the ground (dashed guides show it). Ortho keeps lines and dimensions straight.
  5. Sketch and paint. Line and Pen draw freely in the current color. Paint recolors any part you tap. Tap Paint or Pen again to pick a color, texture or pen size.
  6. Front and Plan. The switch at the top left flips between the front of the house and its floor plan. Each has its own tools, and rooms show their floor area as you size them. They are linked: a ground-floor window or door placed on the front wall also goes into the plan's front wall (the bottom edge of the rooms), and the other way round. Moving or resizing it in one view moves it in the other, and dotted lines show where the other view's walls and openings fall.
  7. Lot. Menu, then Lot, sets the property: a common US lot size or your own width and depth, with setbacks. The plan shows property lines, setbacks, the sidewalk and street; the front shows the property lines; 3D shows the yard. The badge at the top left shows the lot area and how much of it the house covers.
  8. Library. Ready-made windows, doors, roofs, trim and plants for the front, and doors, windows, appliances, bathroom fixtures and furniture for the plan. Pick one, then tap the board to place it.
  9. Measure. Measure drags a dimension between two points. Drag its middle handle to move it away from the wall.
  10. Move around. Pinch to zoom and drag with two fingers, or use the + and − buttons. The corner button fits the drawing to the screen.
