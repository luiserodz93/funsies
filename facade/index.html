How to draw

Every part snaps to real sizes, so the facade you draw is a measured drawing. Sizes are in feet and inches (metres are in the menu).

  1. The plan is the house. Rooms in the floor plan set where the walls stand, and their windows and doors are the house's openings. The front view and 3D are built from the plan, so a change in any view shows in the others. Make a room open (a porch, marquesina, terrace or balcony) by tapping it twice in Plan. Tap the house's walls or roof on the front to change their finish, the number of floors or the roof style.
  2. Floors and stairs. In Plan, Floor 1 2 + at the top picks the floor you're drawing. Tap + to start a floor above: the floor below shows faintly so rooms line up, and you can draw its rooms or start with a room over each room below. Put stairs on the lower floor with the Stairs tool; they show on the floor above as the way down, and climb between the floors in 3D.
  3. Merge rooms and take out walls. Turn on Multi, tap two or more rooms that touch, then tap Merge. If there's more than one wall between them, tap the walls to keep or take out, then Merge: they become one room, like a hallway or a great room. To take out a single wall and keep both rooms' names, tap the wall between them in Plan, then Take out this wall. Split, or Put the wall back, undoes it.
  4. Rooms of any shape. With Room, turn on Any shape and tap each corner of the room: an L, a T or any outline with square corners. Tap the first corner to close it. Each side shows its length as you go.
  5. Build in 3D. Drag to turn the house and pinch to zoom. Tap a wall, then Add a window or Add a door, or pick Window or Door below and tap a wall. Tap a window or door to slide it, resize it, change its style or delete it, and tap the roof to change its style.
  6. Place parts. Pick Window, Door or another part, then tap the board to drop one, or drag to draw it at any size. A window or door placed on the house's wall goes into the plan. A wall drawn beside the house adds a room, and a wall drawn on top adds a story.
  7. Select and adjust. With Select, tap a part, drag it to move, and drag its round handles to resize. Tap a part twice, or the sliders button, to type exact sizes, change its style, texture and color, repeat it, mirror it or add its dimensions.
  8. Select several. With Select, drag across empty board to box-select, or turn on Multi and tap parts to add them. Then move them together, group them, align and distribute them, or copy and paste them into the other view or another design.
  9. Snap. Grid snaps to the grid size, and while you draw in Plan the grid shows over the rooms. Snap lines parts up with the corners, edges and centers of other parts and the ground (dashed guides show it, and a ring marks where a room starts). Ortho keeps lines and dimensions straight.
  10. Sketch and paint. Line and Pen draw freely in the current color. Paint recolors any part you tap. Tap Paint or Pen again to pick a color, texture or pen size.
  11. Front, Plan and 3D. The switch at the top left flips between the front of the house, its floor plan and 3D. Each has its own tools, and rooms show their floor area as you size them. Each floor of the plan has its own rooms, windows, doors and furniture.
  12. Building style. Menu, then Building style, picks how the house is built: in Puerto Rico a concrete house (stucco, flat roof with a parapet, jalousies, rejas, a marquesina), a criollo house or a wood casa de campo on posts; on the US mainland Craftsman, Colonial, Ranch, Modern farmhouse or Modern. New parts start in that style, Restyle this house changes what's drawn, and the Library and New design have parts and starter houses for each. Rooms named Marquesina, Balcón, Terraza, Porch or Carport start open, with a roof and no outside walls.
  13. Lot. Menu, then Lot, sets the property: a common US lot or a Puerto Rico urbanización or your own width and depth, with setbacks. The plan shows property lines, setbacks, the sidewalk and street; the front shows the property lines; 3D shows the yard. The badge at the top left shows the lot area and how much of it the house covers.
  14. Library. Ready-made windows, doors, roofs, trim and plants for the front, and doors, windows, appliances, bathroom fixtures and furniture for the plan. Pick one, then tap the board to place it.
  15. Measure. Measure drags a dimension between two points. Drag its middle handle to move it away from the wall.
  16. Move around. Pinch to zoom and drag with two fingers, or use the + and − buttons. The corner button fits the drawing to the screen.
