How to draw

Every part snaps to real sizes, so the facade you draw is a measured drawing. Sizes are in metres or feet, set in the menu.

  1. Place parts. Pick Wall, Roof, Window or another part, then tap the board to drop one, or drag to draw it at any size. A roof tapped above a wall fits itself to that wall.
  2. Select and adjust. With Select, tap a part, drag it to move, and drag its round handles to resize. Tap a part twice, or the sliders button, to type exact sizes, change its style, texture and color, repeat it or mirror it.
  3. Snap. Grid snaps to the grid size. Snap lines parts up with the edges and centers of other parts and the ground (dashed guides show it). Ortho keeps lines and dimensions straight.
  4. Sketch and paint. Line and Pen draw freely in the current color. Paint recolors any part you tap. Tap Paint or Pen again to pick a color, texture or pen size.
  5. Measure. Measure drags a dimension between two points. Drag its middle handle to move it away from the wall.
  6. Move around. Pinch to zoom, drag with two fingers, or drag empty board with Select. The corner button fits the drawing to the screen.
